# Returns Policy

Harrow & Finch Gifts Ltd (fictional). Version 4.2, effective 1 January 2011. This policy is synthetic: it was written for the RetailOps Agent project and doesn't describe a real business. It adds to customers' statutory rights and doesn't replace them (see the UK Consumer Rights Summary).

## 1. Scope and definitions

### 1.1 Who this policy covers
This policy covers every order placed through our website, by telephone or by email, by retail customers and trade (wholesale) account holders. Where a section applies to only one group, it says so. Orders placed through third-party marketplaces follow that marketplace's returns process, but the time limits in this policy still apply as a minimum.

### 1.2 Retail and trade customers
A retail customer is an individual buying for personal use. A trade customer holds a trade account or places an order with a merchandise value of £500 or more for resale. Trade customers are businesses, so the statutory consumer cancellation rights described in the UK Consumer Rights Summary do not apply to them. Their returns are governed by section 6 of this policy.

### 1.3 Delivery date
All return windows are counted in calendar days starting the day after the order is delivered. If we can't confirm the delivery date, we use the invoice date plus five days for UK orders and the invoice date plus ten days for international orders.

### 1.4 Cancellations are not returns
An order cancelled before dispatch is reversed on our system with a cancellation invoice (the invoice number begins with the letter C). The customer is refunded automatically and nothing needs to be sent back. Staff should not treat a cancellation invoice as evidence that goods were returned, and should not count cancellations when reporting return rates.

## 2. Change-of-mind returns (retail customers)

### 2.1 Standard return window
Retail customers may return any unwanted item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. This is longer than the 14-day statutory cancellation period and is offered as a goodwill policy. Items must be unused, in their original packaging and in a resaleable condition.

### 2.2 Extended Christmas returns
Orders delivered between 1 November and 24 December inclusive can be returned until 31 January of the following year, or 30 days after delivery if that is later. This extension applies to retail customers only and exists so that gifts can be exchanged after the holiday period.

### 2.3 Return postage for change-of-mind returns
The customer pays the cost of returning unwanted items unless the order value was £50 or more, in which case we provide a prepaid returns label on request. We refund the original standard delivery charge only if the entire order is returned. Express or named-day delivery surcharges are not refunded for change-of-mind returns.

### 2.4 Condition of returned items
If a returned item shows signs of use beyond what is needed to inspect it, as a customer would in a shop, we may reduce the refund to reflect the loss in value. The reduction is capped at 50% of the item price, and the customer is told the reason in writing before the refund is processed.

### 2.5 Exchanges
Customers may exchange an item for a different colour or design of the same price within the same window as a return. Where the replacement costs more, the customer pays the difference; where it costs less, we refund the difference. We cover postage for the replacement item.

## 3. Refunds

### 3.1 Refund method
Refunds are made to the original payment method. We can't refund to a different card or account. Where the original payment was a gift voucher, the refund is issued as a new voucher with the same expiry date.

### 3.2 Refund timing
We refund within 14 days of receiving the returned goods at our warehouse, or within 14 days of the customer providing proof of postage, whichever is earlier. For faulty goods we refund within 14 days of agreeing that the customer is entitled to a refund. Banks may take a further three to five working days to show the credit.

### 3.3 Partial returns of sets and bundles
Items sold as a set (for example "set of 6 cake cases" or a gift bundle) must be returned as a complete set for a full refund. Where part of a set is faulty, we refund or replace the faulty part only, priced as the set price divided by the number of items in it.

### 3.4 Promotional and discounted items
Items bought at a discount are refunded at the price actually paid. Free promotional gifts must be returned with the qualifying item or their retail value may be deducted from the refund.

## 4. Faulty, damaged and incorrect items

### 4.1 Items damaged in transit
Customers should report damage in transit within 48 hours of delivery, with photographs of the item and the outer packaging. We will send a replacement or refund in full, including all delivery charges, and the customer does not need to return the damaged item unless we ask. Reports made after 48 hours are handled as faulty goods under section 4.2.

### 4.2 Faulty goods
Faulty goods can be rejected for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Between 30 days and six months after delivery, we'll offer a repair or replacement first; if that fails or isn't possible, the customer can choose a full or partial refund. After six months, we may ask for evidence that the fault was present at delivery. These terms match the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and apply to retail customers whatever section 2 says.

### 4.3 Incorrect items
If we sent the wrong item, we'll collect it at our cost and send the correct item, or refund in full if the customer prefers. Incorrect items reported at any time within 30 days of delivery are handled this way.

### 4.4 Return costs for faulty goods
We pay all return postage for faulty, damaged or incorrect items, and we refund the full original delivery charge.

## 5. Non-returnable items

### 5.1 Excluded categories
The following items cannot be returned for change of mind, although statutory rights for faulty goods still apply:
- personalised or made-to-order items, including engraved gifts;
- confectionery, food and other perishable items;
- earrings and other pierced jewellery where the hygiene seal has been broken;
- gift vouchers and e-gift cards;
- items marked "final sale" or "clearance" at the time of purchase.

### 5.2 Clearance items
Clearance items are marked on the product page and on the invoice. They may be returned only if they are faulty. The standard 30-day window and the Christmas extension don't apply to clearance items.

## 6. Trade (wholesale) customers

### 6.1 Trade return window
Trade customers may return unopened, full cartons within 14 days of delivery. Opened cartons and part cartons can't be returned unless the goods are faulty.

### 6.2 Restocking fee
Change-of-mind trade returns carry a restocking fee of 10% of the invoice value of the returned goods, deducted from the credit. Returns caused by our error carry no fee.

### 6.3 Credit notes
Trade returns are credited to the trade account as a credit note, not refunded to the payment card, unless the account is being closed. Credit notes are valid for 12 months.

### 6.4 Faulty trade goods
Trade customers must report faulty goods within 30 days of delivery. We'll replace faulty items or credit the account at our choice. Our liability to trade customers is limited to the invoice value of the affected goods.

## 7. International returns

### 7.1 International change-of-mind returns
Customers outside the United Kingdom may return items within 30 days of delivery. The Christmas extension in section 2.2 also applies. The customer pays return postage and must mark the parcel as "returned goods" to avoid import charges.

### 7.2 Duties and taxes
Import duties, local taxes and brokerage fees paid by an international customer on the original delivery are not refundable by us. The customer may be able to reclaim them from their local customs authority.

## 8. How to return an item

### 8.1 Starting a return
Customers start a return by emailing the order number and the stock codes of the items to our customer service team. An order number is required; we can't process returns without proof of purchase. We reply with a returns authorisation number within two working days.

### 8.2 Returns authorisation
Every parcel must show the returns authorisation number. Parcels without one may be delayed by up to 14 days while we identify the order.

### 8.3 Disputes and escalation
If a customer is unhappy with a returns decision, the case is escalated to the customer service manager, who responds within five working days. Customers can also use the alternative dispute resolution service named on our website.
